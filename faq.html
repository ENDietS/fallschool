Registration & fees

How do I register for the ENDietS Gather to Nourish School?

Registration is completed through the ICONSD2026 registration platform. Participants must select the option that explicitly includes the ENDietS Gather to Nourish School.

Please note that ICONSD2026 also offers conference-only registration options, which do not include the ENDietS Fall School.

Register through ICONSD2026

Is the Fall School registration separate from ICONSD2026?

No. The Fall School takes place alongside ICONSD2026, and participants register through the ICONSD2026 platform. Please select the combined registration option that includes the ENDietS Fall School.

Does my registration include both ICONSD2026 and the ENDietS Fall School?

Yes, if you choose the ICONSD2026 + ENDietS Fall School registration option. Conference-only registration does not include access to the ENDietS Fall School programme.

What are the registration fees?

Undergraduate students:
Early Bird: €100
Regular: €130

Regular participants (non-undergraduate):
Early Bird: €150
Regular: €190

HDNA members:
Early Bird: €130
Regular: €160

Early Bird registration has been extended until 31 October 2026. Regular registration applies from 1 November 2026.
